What SHOULD stay on this screen (this is important)

This screen is about inputs, constraints, and uncertainty — not outcomes.

✅ Keep these

Purchase price

Square footage

Neighborhood descriptors (“Suburban, Good Schools”)

Rent range (not net income)

Condition (“Minor Updates Needed”)

Financing terms (rates, down payment)

Contractor options (cost vs time)

Cost of diligence (walkthrough)

These are ingredients, not conclusions.

What should be explicitly hidden (even subtly)
❌ Remove or never show here

“Estimated Monthly Cash Flow”

“Potential Profit”

“Likely ROI”

“Expected Net Income”

Any green/red deal signal

Even blurred or “locked” versions on this screen are risky.
This screen should feel pre-analytical, not analytical.

What to show instead (this is the teaching move)

You replace outcome ranges with uncertainty + responsibility cues.

Replace outcome previews with:
1. Outcome Locked Panel

On the right side, where the eye naturally goes:

🔒 Deal Outcome Unknown
Build a pro forma to determine cash flow and risk.

This reinforces:

You are responsible

The game will not save you from bad assumptions

2. Risk Signals (non-numeric)

You can teach without numbers here:

Examples:

“Rent sensitivity: Medium”

“Timeline risk: Elevated”

“Leverage sensitivity: High with Hard Money”

These hint where mistakes happen without giving answers.

3. Explicit Call to Action

Your “View Pro Forma” button should be visually emphasized more than “Make Offer”.

This is critical.

Primary CTA: View Pro Forma
Secondary CTA: Make Offer (dangerous, impulsive)

That subtly teaches discipline.

Why this matters for learning (this is the heart of it)

If players ever succeed without building a pro forma, the game has failed its mission.

By withholding outcome ranges:

Players must think in components

They learn how sensitive outcomes are

They experience the pain of bad assumptions after committing

The postmortem becomes meaningful

This mirrors real life painfully well.

One nuance (important)
Rent range is OK
Net income range is NOT

Why?

Rent is:

A market input

Observational

External

Net income is:

Constructed

Dependent on assumptions

The lesson itself

That distinction is the philosophical backbone of your game.

Final verdict (clear and firm)

Yes. This screen should NOT give any range of potential net income, cash flow, or profit.

It should:

Show ingredients

Show tradeoffs

Show uncertainty

Force the pro forma as the only path to truth
