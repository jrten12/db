The core mistake on the current screen (clear diagnosis)

Right now the screen implicitly says:

“Start with rent, then see if the deal works.”

That’s backwards and dangerous.

In real underwriting, the flow is:

Acquisition price & capital structure

Total basis

Operating assumptions

Cash flow & returns

Sensitivity & fragility

Starting with “Expected Rent” trains optimism first.
You want to train discipline first.

The governing rule for the Pro Forma screen

The pro forma must feel like assembling a machine, not typing guesses.

That means:

The player starts with facts

Then adds assumptions

Then watches consequences emerge visually

What the Pro Forma screen SHOULD be (conceptually)

Think of the screen as three stacked layers, not one long form.

🔧 PRO FORMA SCREEN — CORRECT ARCHITECTURE
LAYER 1: DEAL FOUNDATION (non-negotiable, top section)

This is where the screen should start.

Section title:

Deal Foundation

Fixed / prefilled (from market):

Purchase Price (locked)

Closing Costs (est.) (editable within range)

Initial Rehab Estimate (range midpoint) (editable later)

All-in Basis (live calculated) ← big number, emphasized

📌 This immediately teaches basis, which most beginners never internalize.

Visual treatment:

Large number tiles

Subtle animation when basis changes

No judgment yet

LAYER 2: CAPITAL STACK (financing before rent)
Section title:

Capital Stack

Instead of “Financing: Bank Loan (5%)”, do this:

Financing Path

Conventional

DSCR

Hard Money

Cash / Partner (later)

Once chosen, show sliders or bounded inputs:

Loan % (LTV)

Interest Rate (range)

Points

Amortization / IO toggle

Time to close (affects timeline risk)

Live outputs (graphical):

Monthly debt service

Cash invested at close

Leverage meter (low → high)

📌 This teaches that financing is a design choice, not a dropdown.

LAYER 3: OPERATIONS & ASSUMPTIONS (rent belongs here)

Now — and only now — does rent appear.

Section title:

Operating Assumptions

Inputs:

Market Rent (player chooses within comp range)

Vacancy slider

Taxes

Insurance

Maintenance %

Management toggle

But here’s the key improvement:

Show a Net Operating Income (NOI) bar forming live

Rent comes in from the left

Vacancy subtracts

Expenses peel off visually

NOI emerges as a bar at the end

📌 This teaches flow, not math.

LAYER 4: TIMELINE & RISK (often ignored, critical)
Section title:

Time & Risk

Inputs:

Rehab duration

Contingency %

Holding period

Visual:

Timeline bar (weeks)

Holding cost burn meter

“Delay sensitivity” indicator

📌 This is where your game becomes different from spreadsheets.

LAYER 5: OUTCOMES (graphical, instant, earned)

Only after the above are assembled do outcomes appear.

Outcomes panel (right side, animated):

Monthly Cash Flow (big)

Cash-on-Cash Return

Cap Rate

Fragility warning:

“One delay breaks this deal”

“Rent miss of $100 flips outcome”

These update live as sliders move.

No “Calculate Cash Flow” button needed — that button is a relic.
The dopamine is in watching causality.

Why this fixes everything
Educationally

Teaches basis before yield

Teaches leverage as a choice

Teaches NOI as a process

Teaches fragility, not just returns

Psychologically

Player feels like they are building something

Numbers feel earned

Mistakes feel understandable

Passing on a deal feels intelligent

Game-wise

Sliders = dopamine

Bars filling / shrinking = feedback

Warnings = tension

No spoilers = integrity
