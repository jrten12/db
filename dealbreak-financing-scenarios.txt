DEALBREAK: FINANCING SCENARIOS REFERENCE
=========================================

ASSUMPTIONS
-----------
Hard money: 3% loan fee, 3% closing costs
Bank loan: 1% loan fee, 3% closing costs
Rehab financed
Holding 6 months
ARV for 25% flip ROI
40% rent expenses, 7% refi interest
Total cash needed includes down payment + loan fees + closing + holding costs


HARD MONEY PATH (15% Down, 85% LTC)
-----------------------------------
Max Purchase Price: ~$135,000
Rehab (Financed): $25,000
Total Project Cost: ~$160,000

Holding Time: 6 months
Interest During Rehab: 12% (interest-only)
Loan Fee (3% of loan): ~$4,080
Closing Costs (3% of purchase): ~$4,050

Total Cash Needed (Down + Fees + Closing + Holding): ~$47,630
Initial Cash to Close: ~$35,000

ARV (supports 25% ROI): $225,000

Refi Loan: 75% LTV @ 7% (30-yr)
Refi Loan Amount: ~$168,750
Hard Money Payoff: ~$136,000

Cash Out at Refi: ~$32,750
Cash Left in Deal After Refi: ~$21,500

Rent Expense Ratio: 40%
Minimum Rent for 10% Annual ROI: ~$1,950/month
Implied Rent-to-ARV: ~0.87%

Gameplay Note:
High leverage path. Shorter hold improves ROI, but ARV and rent must hit.

GAME IMPLICATIONS
-----------------
1. Starting cash should be $50,000-60,000 (enough for 1 deal + buffer)
2. Properties should be priced in the $100K-$150K range
3. Hard money = higher risk, higher leverage, needs higher ARV
4. Bank loan = lower risk, more cash tied up, lower ARV needed
5. Cash needed is NOT just down payment - loan fees, closing, holding add up
6. BRRRR model: Cash out at refi can fund next deal if executed well
