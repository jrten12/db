## The Confusion: Cash Out vs. Debt Taken

When I said "you need $44,470" - that's **CASH out of pocket**, but you're also taking on **$116,000 in DEBT**.

Let me show the complete picture:

---

## Example: Oakwood Cottage ($145,000)

### STEP 1: The Purchase (What Happens at Closing)

```
Purchase Price:                    $145,000

FINANCING BREAKDOWN:
├─ Your Cash (Down Payment 20%):   $29,000  ← CASH OUT
└─ Bank's Money (Loan 80%):       $116,000  ← DEBT ADDED

ADDITIONAL CASH REQUIRED:
├─ Loan Origination Fee (2% of loan): $2,320  ← CASH OUT
├─ Closing Costs (3% of purchase):    $4,350  ← CASH OUT
└─ Total Cash at Closing:            $35,670  ← TOTAL CASH OUT

DEBT CREATED:
└─ Loan Balance:                    $116,000
└─ Interest Rate:                      6.5%
└─ Monthly Payment:                    $733
    (This is for 30-year amortization)
```

**After Purchase:**
- You OWN a $145,000 property
- You OWE the bank $116,000
- Your EQUITY is $29,000
- Your CASH is down by $35,670

---

### STEP 2: The Rehab (Weeks 1-4)

```
Rehab Budget:                       $8,000  ← CASH OUT
Contingency (10%):                    $800  ← CASH OUT
Total Rehab:                        $8,800  ← CASH OUT

DURING REHAB (4 months holding):
Monthly Interest on Loan:             $629/month
  ($116,000 × 6.5% ÷ 12 months)

4 Months of Interest:               $2,516  ← CASH OUT
  (Note: This is interest-only during construction)

Property Taxes (4 months):            $800  ← CASH OUT
Insurance (4 months):                 $400  ← CASH OUT

Total Holding Costs:                $3,716  ← CASH OUT
```

**Total CASH Spent So Far:**
```
Down Payment + Fees:    $35,670
Rehab:                  $8,800
Holding Costs:          $3,716
─────────────────────────────
TOTAL CASH OUT:        $48,186
```

**Debt Balance:**
```
Original Loan:         $116,000
Interest Accrued:       $2,516
TOTAL OWED:           $118,516  ← DEBT YOU STILL OWE
```

---

### STEP 3: The Sale (Week 4)

```
SALE PRICE (best case):           $171,000

PAYOFFS:
├─ Loan Payoff:                  -$118,516  ← PAY OFF DEBT
├─ Real Estate Commission (6%):   -$10,260  ← SELLING COST
├─ Closing Costs (seller):         -$2,000  ← SELLING COST
└─ Net Proceeds to YOU:           $40,224  ← CASH BACK TO YOU
```

---

### STEP 4: Calculate Your Profit/Loss

```
CASH YOU PUT IN:
  Down + Fees:          $35,670
  Rehab:                 $8,800
  Holding Costs:         $3,716
  ─────────────────────────────
  TOTAL CASH OUT:      $48,186

CASH YOU GOT BACK:
  Sale Proceeds:       $40,224

YOUR PROFIT/LOSS:
  $40,224 - $48,186 = -$7,962  ❌ YOU LOST MONEY
```

## Why This Matters: The Debt Trap

### You might think:
"I'm buying a $145K house and selling for $171K, that's $26K profit!"

### But the REAL math is:
```
Sale Price:                        $171,000
Minus Loan Payoff:                -$118,516
Minus Selling Costs:               -$12,260
Net Proceeds:                       $40,224

Your Cash Investment:               $48,186
NET RESULT:                         -$7,962 LOSS
```

**The debt doesn't disappear - it gets paid off from your sale proceeds!**

```

─────────────────────────────────
```

**Why the difference?**
