Which due diligence items would you like to complete?
-Investigate market rents — Explore real estate sites like Zillow, Rentometer, Redfin etc to determine rents of similar units in the area; Cost = 1 week *pay for this only in time and not money
-Investigate market sales prices — Look at comparable renovated homes (comps) in the area via real estate sites or your real estate agent and determine what the house might be worth after you fix it up. This is known as After Repair Value (ARV); Cost ‎ = 1 week *pay for this only in time and not money
-Investigate taxes and insurance — Verify that the listed taxes are accurate and complete and call your insurance broker for a quote; Cost ‎ = 1 week
-Complete contractor walk through — Hire a contractor to walk through the property with you, look for condition issues, determine your scope of work and determine your estimated repair budget; Cost ‎ = 1 week & $150
-Complete property inspection — Hire a property inspector to thoroughly evaluate all property systems and look for hidden repair issues; Cost ‎ = 1 week & $750
-Title Report (Required for purchasing property); Cost = 3 weeks & $250
